Why I built this

Two salons, an academy, and a supplies brand. Built by an artist who started at the chair.

I am Amy Williams. I started in beauty in 2006. By 2014 I had opened two physical salons in Brisbane (West End and Boondall) and launched my own supplies line, LashXtd Pro, because the kits on the market were not built for the way I wanted to work.

In 2019 I launched the online academy because half my booked weeks were students flying in for 1-on-1 training. Today I have trained over 25,000 lash artists globally, and Lash Prodigy was named Best Online Brow and Lash Training Business Australia in 2024 by Inside Industry.

The Accelerator exists because every week I get the same DM. "Amy, can I work with you 1-on-1 on the business side." Until now the answer has been no, because I was running it as one-off mentoring with no structure. So I put it together as a real programme. Twelve weeks. Small cohort. Application only.
